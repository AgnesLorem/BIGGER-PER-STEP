## Scope

### Game Feel Engine (Client-Side)
- **CameraShakeController**: Scale-adaptive camera shake on Stomp, Rebirth, Level Up
- **SoundController**: Adaptive SFX with pitch scaling (1.0 → 0.4 based on VisualScale)
- **FloatingFeedbackController**: Pop-up numbers (+Bigger, CRUSH!, LEVEL UP!, REBIRTH!) with Object Pooling
- **VFXController**: Stomp shockwave, Rebirth golden burst, Level Up sparkle particles

### Multi-Tier Aura System (Server-Authoritative)
- 4 tiers: Green Ki (10D, x1.15) → Blue Ki (30D, x1.30) → Golden Ki (60D, x1.50) → God Ki (100D, x1.75)
- Passive auto-unlock at Destruction milestones
- Cosmetic-buff decoupled: buff always uses highest unlocked tier
- Server-replicated particle VFX (all players see Auras)
- Wardrobe GUI for cosmetic selection + VFX toggle
- Persists through Rebirth (never reset)

## Mathematical Summary

### Aura Multiplier
- **Buff Rule:** Always applies highest unlocked tier, regardless of cosmetic selection
- **Formula:** `AuraMult = HighestUnlockedTier.Multiplier` (or 1.0 if none)
- **Total Growth Chain:** `EquipmentMult × RebirthMult × AuraMult × GamePasses`

### SFX Pitch Scaling
- `Pitch = clamp(1.0 - (VisualScale - 1) × 0.004, 0.4, 1.0)`

### Camera Shake Magnitudes
- Stomp: `min(VisualScale × 0.15, 3.0)` studs, 0.35s, 20Hz
- Rebirth: `2.0` studs (fixed), 0.6s, 15Hz
- Level Up: `0.3` studs, 0.2s, 25Hz
