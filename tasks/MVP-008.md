## Architectural & Mathematical Summary

### 1. Level Curve (Quadratic)
- **Formula:** $\text{Bigger}(L) = \left\lfloor 1.66204986 \times (L - 1)^2 \right\rfloor$
- **Constant:** $\text{BASE\_REQUIREMENT} = 600 / (19 \times 19) \approx 1.66205$
- **Target:** Level 20 = 600 Bigger (5 minutes walking at 2 Bigger/sec)
- **Inverse:** $\text{Level} = \left\lfloor \sqrt{\text{Bigger} / \text{BASE\_REQUIREMENT}} \right\rfloor + 1$ with discrete monotonic correction

### 2. Character Visual Scale (Power Curve)
- **Formula:** $\text{Scale}(L) = 1 + 0.080739 \times \max(0, L - 1)^{0.8299}$
- **Config:** `Formula = "power"`, `Coefficient = 0.080739`, `Exponent = 0.8299`, `MaxVisualScale = 200`
- **Key Milestones:** Level 10 ≈ 1.50x, Level 50 ≈ 3.04x, Level 100 ≈ 4.66x, Level 500 ≈ 15.00x

### 3. Rebirth Progression System (Linear Multiplier)
- **Level Requirement:** $\text{ReqLevel}(R) = 20 + R \times 50$ (Level-only gate)
  - Rebirth 1 (R0→R1): Level 20 (~5 min walking)
  - Rebirth 2 (R1→R2): Level 70
  - Rebirth 3 (R2→R3): Level 120
  - Rebirth 4 (R3→R4): Level 170
  - Rebirth 5 (R4→R5): Level 220
  - Rebirth 10 (R9→R10): Level 470
- **Linear Multiplier Curve:** $\text{Mult}(R) = 1 + R \times 0.75$
  - R0: 1.00x | R1: 1.75x | R2: 2.50x | R3: 3.25x | R5: 4.75x | R10: 8.50x | R20: 16.00x
- **Rebirth Reset:** Bigger = 0, Level = 1, VisualScale = 1.0x, GrowthRemainder = 0
- **Rebirth Kept:** Growth Upgrades, Destruction, Portal Progress
- **Network Remote:** `BiggerEvents/Rebirth/Request` (Client → Server), `Response` (Server → Client)
- **Rate Limit:** 2s debounce cooldown (`MinimumRebirthCooldownSeconds`)

### 4. Growth Upgrades Simplified
- **Gates:** Removed `RequiredLevel` entirely; unlock requires only `RequiredDestruction` milestone and sequence predecessor.
- **Destruction Milestones:** Protein (1), SilverProtein (3), GoldProtein (8), DiamondProtein (15), GrowthSerum (30), AdvancedSerum (60), TitanSerum (100).

### 5. World 1 Portal
- **RequiredLevel:** Changed from 5 to 8 (reachable in ~1.5 minutes walking).
